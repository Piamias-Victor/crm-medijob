# Intérim V1 = Weekly availability + full Badakan read, not the 4-workflow PDF

Mathieu’s PDF lists four operational workflows (inscription/validation with relances, weekly availability, reminder engine, mission search). The agreed V1 is the durable process: **Weekly availability** (calendar-grid personal page + CRM filters) plus a **complete Badakan read model** (inscrits and app-validated profiles, missions, pharmacies, Badakan comments, `SEARCH_APPLIED` applicants, and Badakan contracts). The availability page shows **one week at a time**, with navigation to following weeks; **full history of filled weeks is kept**. Tests use a copyable secret URL that **stays valid** and opens a **public** page (no Candidate account; unguessable token). **Auto-send of that link is in V1, last, by one SMS at App-validated** (not a weekly cron). If the phone is missing, wait until a later sync provides it, then send once. Reminder engine (J+2/5/10), « email when app-validated », and client notifs on applications remain after that core. CRM filters for positioning are **slot + JobTitle + city/radius**, on App-origin App-validated Candidates who are not Inactif — not full CVthèque matching (software, salary, contracts). Badakan data is pulled on the **same periodic cycle as Profils app** (no manual refresh button).

Partial Badakan sync in V1 was rejected: it would cheapen the first invoice little and force a schema revisit. The Finance tab « Intérim » (Lignes de suivi) is unrelated. The CRM does not POST `/comments` to Badakan. **V1 is read-only toward Badakan** (no staff, validate, mission PUT, or contract writes); staffing stays in the app/admin.
